## Releasetext für GitHub (Titel: „Pronunciation Matters 1.0.0 – First Stable Release“)

Gegenüber dem Entwurf präzisiert: Unterricht (nur Spanisch ausgearbeitet), Designdokumentation (Spanisch, Englisch).

> Version 1.0.0 marks the first stable release of **Pronunciation Matters**, a multilingual platform developed at the University of Marburg that connects empirical research on learner pronunciation with linguistically informed resources for language teaching.
>
> **Research:** Learner pronunciation corpora for Spanish, French, English, and German provide authentic recordings of learners and reference speakers. The platform documents the corpus design (currently for Spanish and English), offers controlled access to the research data, and provides interactive tools for pronunciation comparison.
>
> **Teaching:** Topic pages for Spanish introduce linguistic concepts, illustrate pronunciation phenomena through authentic audio examples, and provide practical ideas for classroom teaching. Topic pages for English, French, and German have been announced.
>
> The platform features a bilingual English–German interface, role-based access to research data, and a structured framework for scholarly documentation and citation.
>
> **Version 1.0.0 establishes the platform as a stable foundation for ongoing research and teaching.** Its corpora, analytical features, and teaching resources will continue to evolve.
>
> Changelog: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/FTacke/promat-webapp/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
>
> Project lead and development: Prof. Dr. Felix Tacke, University of Marburg.
>
> https://pronunciation-matters.de
