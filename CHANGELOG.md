# Changelog

Versions of the Pronunciation Matters platform software. Corpus data and teaching content grow continuously and are not
versioned by these releases. Release procedure: `docs/runbooks/deploy-and-rollback.md`.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-11-08 – First Stable Release

### Platform

- First stable release of Pronunciation Matters, in regular operation at https://pronunciation-matters.de.
- Bilingual German and English interface.
- Responsive design with light and dark themes.
- Research and teaching sections for Spanish, French, English and German.

### Research

- Learner pronunciation corpora for Spanish, French, English and German with recordings of learners and reference
  speakers.
- Public documentation of the corpus design for Spanish (German and English) and English (English); French and German
  are in preparation.
- Speaker profiles, an audio player, pronunciation comparison and phenomenon collections for registered users.
- Login, access requests, personal and group accounts and role-based access to research data.

### Teaching

- Seven Spanish topic pages with linguistic explanations, curated authentic audio examples, listening comparisons and
  classroom suggestions, also available in a Spanish content edition.
- Announced topic pages for English (5), French (6) and German (4).

### Scholarly infrastructure

- Attribution and citation information for the platform, the corpora, the design articles and the teaching pages,
  with machine-readable metadata.
- Public corpus design pages; all other research pages and research audio require authorised access.

### Release status

- First stable platform release.
- Corpora and teaching resources will continue to be expanded.
